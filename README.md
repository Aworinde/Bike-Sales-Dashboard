## Business Objective
To analyze customer demographics and behavioral factors that influence bike purchases, and to identify key segments, regions, and patterns that drive sales performance.


## Dashboard Overview
![Bike sales Dashboard](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/86aad9bd-f655-41ff-83fd-8b8d321b1eae)


## Key KPIs
* Total Customers: 1,000
* Total Bikes Sold: 481
* Overall Purchase Rate: 48%
* Customer Income Range: $10,000 – $170,000 (Avg: $56,360)
* Customer Age Range: 25 – 89 (Avg: 44)


## Business Questions Addressed
* How does family size (children & cars) impact purchase likelihood?
* Which age groups are most likely to buy bikes?
* How does income level relate to purchase behavior?
* Which regions drive the highest sales volume?
* How does profession influence purchasing decisions?


## Key Insights
* Middle-aged customers (31–54) show the highest purchase volume.
* Customers with 0–1 cars have a higher likelihood of purchasing compared to larger car ownership groups.
* Households with fewer children (0–1) are more likely to purchase bikes than larger families.
* North America is the highest-performing region, followed by Europe and the Pacific region.
* Professional occupations show the strongest positive purchase difference.
* Higher average income is associated with higher purchase likelihood, especially among male customers.
* Customers with shorter commute distances (0–1 miles) show higher engagement and purchase rates.


## Technical Implementation
* Cleaned and standardized raw customer and sales data
* Built PivotTables to aggregate purchase behavior by demographic factors
* Created Power BI–style KPI cards using GETPIVOTDATA
* Designed interactive PivotCharts for trend and segment analysis
* Implemented slicers for dynamic filtering (e.g., marital status, purchase status)
* Applied dashboard layout principles for executive-level readability


## Business Impact
This dashboard enables stakeholders to:
  * Identify high-value customer segments
  * Target marketing efforts by age, profession, and family profile
  * Optimize regional sales strategies
  * Support data-driven decisions for inventory and promotions
